📖 StudyFlow Feature Manual & Page Guide

◫ Overview Page

Your command center uniting daily study priorities, syllabus completion metrics, and upcoming milestones into one cohesive workspace.

📊Overall Syllabus BarCurriculum

Tracks your aggregate topic completion across all academic & GATE subjects. Check off topics in the Subjects view to move this bar forward.

☀️Today's Priorities (Next up)Daily Tasks

Lists urgent daily study tasks due today. Complete tasks directly with the checkbox. Unfinished daily tasks automatically carry forward tomorrow with a missed remark.

🗓️Upcoming DeadlinesMilestones

Strictly monitors long-term overall task milestones, term submissions, and calendar events. Daily tasks are excluded here to keep major deadlines distinct.

⏱️Focus Minutes TodayDeepTick

Shows total minutes of deep focus logged today. Click "Start a focus session" to launch the DeepTick Pomodoro timer immediately.

▤ GATE & Academic Syllabus

Organize every course subject, extract syllabus topics from lecture handouts via AI Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and track mastery topic-by-topic.

＋Add SubjectManagement

Create subjects with code (e.g. CS201), semester/term, and syllabus topic lists. Each topic line becomes an interactive checkbox.

📷Syllabus OCR ImportAI Powered

Upload a photo or screenshot of your syllabus or lecture schedule. Built-in Tesseract OCR automatically reads the text and extracts topic lines into checkboxes.

☑️Topic ChecklistsMastery

Check off topics as you complete lectures, readings, or revisions. Mastery percentages update across the entire app and your Scholar Profile.

✏️Edit & DeleteControls

Use "Edit" to add newly introduced course topics or reorganize units. Deleting a subject cleans up associated topics safely.

✳ Extra Skills Beyond Syllabus

Track practical skills outside traditional academic syllabi (e.g., Python, Cloud, Machine Learning, System Design, Professional Speaking).

🚀Add Skill PlanLearning

Define new skills with learning category, target completion date, current proficiency (Beginner to Advanced), and study notes.

📚Learning Resources HubMaterials

Link courses, YouTube playlists, official documentation, articles, or textbooks. Use Title | Link format to generate clickable launch buttons.

✨Real-Time Milestone LoggingAccomplishment

Instead of rigid pre-planned checklists, log what you learned whenever you grasp an important concept, build a project, or finish a tutorial. Every entry records an achieved milestone with optional key takeaways.

📈Mastery & CompletionProgress

Track your total logged milestones across all skills, monitor skills in progress, and celebrate skills marked as Completed.

▦ Study Calendar & Deadlines

Plan exams, project submissions, class sessions, and deadlines with an equally spaced 7-day grid and selected day agenda.

📊Month Highlights BarMetrics

Instant breakdown of total scheduled events, overall deadlines, and daily tasks due in the active month.

🏷️Filter Chips (All / Events / Deadlines)Filtering

Filter cell badges in real time to isolate 📅 Events, 🟠 Overall Deadlines, or ☀️ Daily tasks without page reloads.

🗓️Equally Spaced 7-Day GridLayout

All Monday–Sunday columns are strictly identical in width. Mini-pills truncate cleanly with "+X more" indicators so the layout never distorts.

📋Selected Day AgendaActionable

Click any day to open its full agenda. Check off tasks inline directly from the calendar, edit event times, or add new items pre-filled to that date.

☑ Tasks & Daily Flow (Daily vs Overall)

StudyFlow provides two dedicated task engines to separate immediate daily study commitments from long-term academic milestones.

☀️Daily TasksImmediate

Tasks committed for today. If any daily task is uncompleted when the day passes, StudyFlow automatically rolls it over to the next day with an explicit ⚠️ Missed & Carried Forward remark so work is never forgotten.

📋Overall TasksMilestones

Master backlog for multi-day assignments, term projects, thesis preparation, and exam dates. These exclusively populate the Overview's Upcoming Deadlines.

🔍Scope Tabs & Status FiltersNavigation

Switch between ☀️ Daily, 📋 Overall, or 📑 All. Filter by Open, Completed, or isolate carried-over tasks with the ⚠️ Carried Over button.

⏰Reminders & Pomo EstimationDeepTick

Set browser push notifications for exact reminder times, attach course subjects, and assign estimated pomodoro focus blocks.

↗ Mock Tests & Paper Review (The Paper Manual)

This system transforms test attempts into academic gains. Upload actual written papers, log question mistakes, and let StudyFlow automatically uncover your weakest syllabus topics.

📄Upload Test PaperPaper Storage

Attach your written exam as a PDF or image (PNG, JPG, WebP). Files are stored offline in browser IndexedDB with automatic thumbnail generation.

👁️Full Paper Lightbox ViewerReader

Click "View paper" on any test card to inspect your full written paper in high resolution with download capabilities and zoom.

❌Mistake Classification LoggerAnalysis

Log mistakes by Question Number, Marks Lost, and Mistake Type (Conceptual, Calculation, Silly / Reading, Time management, Unattempted). Record the correct approach and the key lesson to prevent repeats.

🧠Automated Topic Weakness EngineAnalytics

StudyFlow analyzes mark losses across all tests and ranks syllabus topics by total marks lost, pinpointing exactly which topics need revision first.

💡Exam Strategy & PointersStrategy

Record exam pacing rules, negative marking traps, and actionable guidelines in the dedicated pointers box for each mock test.

✓Resolution WorkflowMastery

Once you thoroughly re-solve and understand a question, mark it "Resolved". The question turns green and updates your revision metrics.

◷ Focus Timer & Pomodoro (DeepTick)

DeepTick delivers science-backed focus intervals with cycle tracking, session presets, and dynamic completion time calculations.

⏱️Session Differentiation PresetsPresets

Choose 15m (Quick Sprint), 25m (Standard Pomodoro), 30m (Study Block), 45m (Deep Session), or 60m (Intense Focus). Clicking any preset instantly sets the clock duration.

🍅Pomodoro Cycle RoundsIntervals

Displays 🍅 Round X of 4 · (Long break after round 4). Each cycle consists of 4 focus rounds separated by 5m short breaks, followed by a rewarding 15m long break after round 4.

📋Assign Focus TasksTracking

Click "Choose Existing Task" to attach a StudyFlow task to your timer, or "+ Add Extra Task" for impromptu work. Tracks completed vs estimated pomodoros.

📊Today's Pomo Goal vs Cycle CountClarity

The footer tracks your daily goal (e.g. 0 / 4 Pomos completed today), while the timer banner tracks your position in the 4-round break cycle.

🏁Dynamic Finish At CalculationEstimation

Calculates the exact wall-clock time you will complete your queued work. Displays --:-- when idle so it never projects imaginary marathon sessions.

👤 Scholar Profile & Performance Analytics

Your scholar dashboard provides visual analytics on study habits, syllabus mastery distributions, and automated study recommendations.

📊Weekly Study Hours ChartGraph

Visual bar breakdown of hours logged Monday through Sunday. Hover over bars to see daily focus times and maintain consistent study velocity.

🎯Subject Mastery BreakdownSyllabus

Visual meters showing topic completion percentages per subject to easily identify neglected course modules.

💡Intelligent Study RecommendationsAI Guidance

Dynamic coaching prompts synthesized from upcoming deadlines, mock test weakest topics, and recent study streaks.
